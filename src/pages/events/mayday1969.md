When this page is complete, it will display an account of the mass strike on 1 May 1969, in which thousands of workers in completely different sectors and workplaces walked out in protest at the [government's proposals](/StrikingSixties/sources/in-place-of-strife) to restrict the right to strike (and perhaps also wider discontent). For now, you can watch this short primary source, a newsreel by British Pathé: note how casually the source treats the event! Or, you can return to the [homepage](/StrikingSixties).<br><br>
